Evidence support

Public chain information · Read only

Polkaswap Evidence explains available SORA transaction data and public-wallet activity. It shows source, freshness and missing-data limits with each supported result.

Verified community route

The official Polkaswap site links the Polkaswap community on Telegram. This is a verified community link, not a dedicated plugin support service or a guarantee of response.

A dedicated plugin support and privacy contact, and the legal operator, still require confirmation for public directory submission.

When evidence is unavailable

Check that you supplied a public SORA2 transaction hash or address. A missing indexed record does not prove that a transaction failed. An unavailable source or an incomplete page does not prove a zero balance or complete history. Retry later when the data source is unavailable.

Current positions and retained snapshots do not establish lifetime profit and loss, annual yield, cost basis or taxable profit.

Share only what is necessary

For a public issue report, describe the requested read, error status and time. A public transaction hash may help, but share an address only if you are comfortable linking it to your report. Do not post seed phrases, private keys, wallet backups, passwords or sign-in tokens.
