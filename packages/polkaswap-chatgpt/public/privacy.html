How Evidence uses data

Read-only preview · 1 October 2026

This page describes the preview application's data handling. The operator, dedicated privacy contact and infrastructure retention must be confirmed before it becomes an approved service privacy policy.

Public identifiers, chosen by you

Evidence accepts a public SORA transaction hash or wallet address and pagination parameters. It uses them to look up transaction evidence, available holdings, liquidity positions and indexed activity. Public addresses can still be linked to individuals.

No wallet connection, seed phrase, private key or signature is needed. Do not submit wallet secrets.

Where a lookup goes

The SORA chain node receives the public address needed for direct holdings reads; portfolio pagination does not require the indexer. Transaction and signer-history identifiers go to the Polkaswap indexer, and liquidity reads combine its pool catalog with chain reads. Results return to ChatGPT or the MCP client. The service receives the arguments of the tool call. The client applies its own conversation and data policies.

Application storage and logs

The application does not persist query bodies, wallet addresses, hashes or result data. Its operational logs contain request method, a coarse route label, status and duration, with no raw wallet identifiers, request bodies or client IP addresses. The configured reverse proxy supplies its connecting client's IP for rate limiting. The service accepts this forwarded address only from its configured local proxy, otherwise using the connection address. It hashes the selected IP with a random process salt and retains one-minute request-count buckets in memory. Expired buckets are removed on a later request; restarting the process clears them.

Portfolio continuation cursors identify the same public account and finalized block and use a temporary server key for validation. They expire after a service restart and do not authorize wallet transactions. Their contents are not encrypted or secret and are not stored in application logs or a database.

The result view uses no analytics, cookies or persistent browser storage.

Infrastructure and upstream services

Network infrastructure, hosting, ChatGPT, the indexer and chain node have separate data handling. Their logging, retention and privacy contacts are not established by this preview disclosure. It does not promise end-to-end anonymity or a service-wide retention period.

Questions

A dedicated plugin privacy contact has not yet been appointed. Visit Evidence support information for the verified Polkaswap community route. An existing Polkaswap privacy policy is linked from the official site; it does not by itself establish this new plugin's operator or practices.
